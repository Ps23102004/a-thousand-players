# Suggestions inbox

Anyone can add a suggestion: features, wording, risks, bugs, "we should check this". **Aman** suggests from the rules and research; **Aashna** from testing and code; Parth too. Add yours **at the top**, copy the template, and keep it short. Parth decides; mark the status when it is decided.

Template:

```
### <short title>  (Aman | Aashna | Parth, date)
- Why: <what problem or rule this answers>
- Evidence: <file path, rule clause, command output, or link>
- Cost: <hours> and risk: <low | medium | high>
- Status: proposed | accepted | done | rejected (reason)
```

Rules of thumb: a suggestion that **removes a risk** or **fixes a claim that is not true** beats a new feature. Anything that adds a claim must come with evidence we can show.

---

### (example) Show model credit balance in the smoke-test checklist  (Aashna, 2026-10-29)
- Why: the rules require the app to stay usable for judges until Dec 15.
- Evidence: REQUIREMENTS.md, section B, "Testing".
- Cost: 15 minutes, low risk.
- Status: proposed
