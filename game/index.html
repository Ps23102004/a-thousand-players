Connect your DualSense by USB or pair it with your computer over Bluetooth. Click the game once to focus it and enable sound, then press ✕. The browser must report a standard-mapped gamepad. Vibration is optional; adaptive triggers are not used.

FIGHT SMART. Tap, release, and tap again to chain strikes; up to three follow-ups queue. Finishers and accumulated hits cause a knockdown. A mount allows three strikes with a new escape chance each time. The rival mixes punches, kicks and throws, remembers recent attacks, and punishes recovery. A clinch allows two strikes before a throw or release. Missing a prompt can let the rival escape or reverse. Holding a key or controller button does not clear prompts. Release between taps.
